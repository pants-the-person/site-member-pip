My dad brought home a Pentium II from work because they got new computers. It has **32 MB of RAM** and a 4 MB video card. It is so fast.

I installed Windows 95 and then my uncle gave me Windows 98. It took all night. The CD-ROM drive makes a sound like a jet engine but it works.

First things I did:

1. Installed the demo of Half-Life
2. Made a shortcut to the internet
3. Set the wallpaper to a picture of a wolf
4. Broke the sound drivers

The sound works again now. I had to go to the control panel and click things until it stopped saying "no."

My old computer is in the closet. I am keeping it for parts. Or maybe I will turn it into a server. My brother says I cannot have two computers. He is wrong.
