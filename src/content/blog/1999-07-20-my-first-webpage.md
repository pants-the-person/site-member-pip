I made this page in the school computer lab. It took four hours because I kept losing my work when the computer froze.

Mr. H helped me with the table. I wanted a background image of a Pokemon but he said it would make the text hard to read. So I used a pattern instead.

I learned how to do links today. You can click "About Me" and it goes to another page. It is like magic.

I also learned that you have to save the file with `.html` at the end or the browser just shows the code. That was a bad hour.

My goal is to get 100 visitors. If you are reading this, you are one of them. Please sign my guestbook so I know you were here.

Next I want to add a MIDI that plays when the page loads. Mr. H says autoplay music is annoying. Mr. H is wrong.
